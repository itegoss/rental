About KYS Bhayander

Dedicated unit of Kutch Yuvak Sangh committing ourselves to the welfare of society.

Who We Are

KYS (Bhayander), a unit of Kutch Yuvak Sangh, is a registered non-governmental organization (NGO) dedicated to empowering communities and transforming lives through its multiple humanitarian, social, educational, and cultural initiatives.

We are driven by the spirit of selfless service and responsibility towards society. Our membership is inclusive and welcomes anyone regardless of caste, gender, or religion. Our volunteers work diligently to ensure we remain a reliable pillar of support for everyone in times of need.

How we do it and Why?

We facilitate medical equipment rentals at low-cost deposits, organize free blood donation camps, manage critical blood requirements, and connect matching donors with hospitals. We believe healthcare support should be accessible and affordable for all families.
